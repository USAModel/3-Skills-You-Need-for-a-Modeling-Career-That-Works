# 3-Skills-You-Need-for-a-Modeling-Career-That-Works
Learn the skills that can push your modeling career forward, and how getting them down leads to real opportunities.

To succeed in modeling, three skills have to be in place: posing, runway walking and communicating well. Posing is how we carry emotion and make an outfit work, while a strong runway walk shows our confidence and our style. Communicating well is what builds the connections that matter in this industry. Concentrating on those areas is what moves our modeling careers along. If we're ready for some tips on growing and networking, there's more to get into.

**Posing Skills: Mastering the Art of Expression**
Thinking about modeling, one of the most essential skills we have to get down is posing, since that's the language we use to carry emotion, mood and style in every shot.
Good posing brings our stories alive and makes an outfit more appealing. To get there, we have to know our bodies, control our expressions and connect with the camera.
Practicing in front of a mirror or a camera lets us try different angles and expressions. Keep at it and posing turns instinctive, so the movements start flowing into each other.

**Runway Walking: Commanding Attention With Confidence**
A runway walk is essential for any aspiring model, since that's our chance to hold the room and show the style that's ours.
With the right technique behind it, we leave a lasting impression on the audience and on clients. Here are three key elements to focus on for a successful runway walk:
Posture and Balance: We should maintain strong posture, as it exudes confidence and elegance.
Balancing our weight properly is vital for fluid movement.
Facial Expression: Keeping a neutral yet confident expression helps us connect with the audience without distracting from the garments.
Arm Movements: Relaxed arm movements can enhance our overall presentation, allowing our outfits to shine.

**Effective Communication: Building Connections in the Industry**
A confident runway walk will only carry us so far in modeling, since communicating well has a pivotal part to play in building connections that last.
We need strong relationships with photographers, designers and other models, and those come out of clear, professional conversation. Listening carefully to direction is what makes a collaboration work, while giving our feedback keeps things productive.
Networking at events widens our circle, and it lets us show how adaptable and approachable we are.

**Frequently Asked Questions**
**What Should I Include in My Modeling Portfolio?**
Building our modeling portfolio, we want a range of high-quality images covering different poses, expressions and styles. Showing our versatility and the look that's ours is what brings clients and agencies in.

**How Can I Stay Healthy While Modeling?**
To stay healthy while modeling, we put balanced nutrition, regular exercise and enough sleep first. Let's build good habits, listen to our bodies, and keep our minds on our well-being and how we perform.

**What Types of Modeling Exist Beyond Fashion?**
There's plenty beyond fashion, like commercial, editorial, fitness and plus-size modeling. Each niche offers something different, and each lets us show our range and reach a different audience in this industry.

**How Do I Handle Rejection in the Modeling Industry?**
Rejection in modeling isn't easy, but we remind ourselves it comes with the territory. Let's take it on, learn from the feedback, and stay positive. Every experience moves us nearer our goals and grows us.
